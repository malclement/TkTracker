# TkTracker 2.1.0

## Install

Unzip `TkTracker-2.1.0.zip` and move `TkTracker.app` to `/Applications`. Then run
`xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine /Applications/TkTracker.app` in Terminal, or open the
app once and choose **Open Anyway** in System Settings › Privacy & Security. This is
needed once, because the build is ad-hoc signed rather than notarized by Apple. It
runs as a menu bar app: look for the chart icon in the status bar.

## One workshop per project

- **Grouped by project.** Every session open on a project now works in the same building, instead of one room per session. The session list groups sessions under their project, with subagents under the session they belong to.
- **Room for large teams.** A building grows along the street two desks at a time, up to 12 desks. Session leads get desks before subagents. Desks never move when someone joins or leaves. Anyone beyond 12 is counted on a "+N" plaque on the nameplate, and everyone is listed in the session list.
- **A room that makes sense.** Desks run along the back wall and in a middle row. By the door there's a lounge: the couch stands against the side wall facing into the room, with a rug, a coffee machine and a whiteboard. Wood floors are laid as planks, other floors as tiles.
- **Smaller changes.** A waiting lead watches one of its own subagents first. The whole lot fits at a larger zoom in smaller windows. The mailbox no longer sits under the nameplate.

## Requirements and limitations

Requires macOS 15 or later. Shortcuts actions are not supported in this ad-hoc build: macOS rejects them without a Developer ID.

App version 2.1.0, build 12. Supports Apple Silicon and Intel. Usage archives from 2.0.x are kept as they are.
