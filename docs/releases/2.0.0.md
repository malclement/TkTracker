# TkTracker 2.0.0

## Install

Unzip `TkTracker-2.0.0.zip` and move `TkTracker.app` to `/Applications`. Then run
`xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine /Applications/TkTracker.app` in Terminal, or open the
app once and choose **Open Anyway** in System Settings › Privacy & Security. This is
needed once, because the build is ad-hoc signed rather than notarized by Apple. It
runs as a menu bar app: look for the chart icon in the status bar.

## Workshops

Watch your agents at work in a pixel-art lot, in the style of a life sim. Each loaded session gets a room by the street; its lead and subagents are little Sims with a plumbob over their heads. Follow one room, or see the whole lot at once.

- Each state has its own place and pose: typing at the desk, standing over a teammate while waiting, napping on the couch when idle, cheering then heading for coffee when a turn completes.
- When an agent needs you, it walks to the front of the room and waves, and the Needs you strip jumps to it.
- Thought bubbles show which kind of tool is running: search, read, edit, run or delegate.
- Desks carry a paper stack that grows with the session's recorded tokens.
- Dark mode is night: streetlamps come on, and desk lamps light up only while their agent works.
- Switch sessions and inspect every subagent from the team list and portrait bar. Filter by project or provider, drag to pan, and explore a clearly labeled demo.
- Rooms appear when sessions are detected and empty when closure is reliably observed. Idle time and turn completion do not close a session.

## Requirements and limitations

Requires macOS 15 or later. Workshop monitoring reads local provider activity while the page is open; it does not install hooks or change provider configuration.

Codex may keep a session loaded after its tab closes, so its room can stay until Codex releases it. Claude Code tracking requires local session metadata. Missing provider signals cannot reliably identify every approval or waiting state. See [workshop lifecycle details](https://github.com/malclement/TkTracker/blob/main/docs/workshops.md).

Shortcuts actions are not supported in this ad-hoc build: macOS rejects them without a Developer ID.

App version 2.0.0, build 9. Supports Apple Silicon and Intel. Usage archives from 1.5.1 are kept and upgraded in place; back up before comparing builds on important history.
